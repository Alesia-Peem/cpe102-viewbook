# Course materials → where they belong

The files in `Downloads/CPE102` were used as the **source** for the course facts on the
site (`content/course.ts`, the `covered` / `tools` lists in `data/syllabus.ts`, and the two
project files). They are **not** published: they are the instructors' material, some are
large (AI & ML is 52 MB), and a public viewbook should link to them only with permission.

## Differences between the syllabus and the slides

- Week 3: the syllabus says *LibreCAD / TinkerCAD*; the slides teach **Onshape**. The site shows the
  syllabus subtitle and lists all three tools.
- The syllabus calls the assignment the "CPE 101 Viewbook"; the course is CPE102.
- The Game Jam guide gives the deadline as 12 November **2026** on its first page and **2025** later; the site uses 2026.
