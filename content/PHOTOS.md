## Adding photos for a week

1. Copy the photos into `public/images/weeks/week-07/` (any of .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .avif).
2. Either leave `photos: []` in `data/weeks.ts` (every photo in the folder is used, sorted by file
   name — name them `01.jpg`, `02.jpg` … to control the order), **or** list the paths you want in
   `photos` and their `captions` in the same order.
3. The week's layout adapts automatically to how many photos it has and whether they are portrait
   or landscape. Nothing else needs to change.
